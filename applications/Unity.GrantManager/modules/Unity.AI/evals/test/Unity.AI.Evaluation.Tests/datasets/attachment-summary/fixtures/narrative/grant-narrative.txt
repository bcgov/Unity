Community Literacy Expansion Program
Requesting Organization: Riverbank Community Foundation
Registration: Registered non-profit society, established 2011.

Program Overview
The Riverbank Community Foundation proposes an 18-month expansion of its
literacy programming into three regional library branches. Programming will
focus on early-childhood read-aloud sessions, adult ESL conversation circles,
and after-school homework support.

Budget Summary
Total budget: $50,000
- Personnel (part-time facilitators): $30,000
- Materials (books, workbooks): $8,000
- Facility rentals and utilities: $6,000
- Evaluation and reporting: $4,000
- Contingency: $2,000

Timeline
Months 1-3: hire facilitators, procure materials.
Months 4-15: deliver weekly sessions at all three branches.
Months 16-18: participant surveys, evaluation, and final report.

Expected Outcomes
At least 120 children served in read-aloud sessions.
At least 40 adults enrolled in ESL circles across the 18 months.
